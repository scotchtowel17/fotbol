# Changes of October 1, 2026: the audit, implemented

Everything from AUDIT-2026-10-01.md's ranked cut list, applied in dependency order with the full suite run green
between phases. Final state: 756 tests pass, `npm run check` green on all 37 scenarios, `npm run sanity` byte-identical
(the engine's answers did not move), zero console errors in a full live play-through, and the live checks below.

## Tier 1: fixes

1. **Touch protection** (css/app.css): `.board-svg` always carries `touch-action: manipulation` (no double-tap zoom
   mid-clip), and `none` whenever drag or tap targets are on, `has-targets` included, so a browser pan gesture can no
   longer eat a kid's tap in "Who's open?" through `pointercancel`.
2. **The Road gate** (js/ui/player/road.js): a node opens when the one before it has a star OR a finished set, and the
   same for a chapter's `opensAfter`. `nextNode` offers the first never-played open node before any replay, so Play
   always moves forward (recall reps keep old ideas warm; replays stay one Road tap away). Verified live: a five-rep
   set finished at 0 stars opened the next chapter and moved Play to "Get Open".
3. **kidscore honesty** (js/engine/kidscore.js, ARCHITECTURE §5.18): `coachMax`/`coachMaxCap` are now
   `coachLift`/`coachLiftTo`, and the doc says the lift only adds stars and never reaches 3. The false "never stricter
   than Coach mode" claim is gone from code comments and ARCHITECTURE (behavior unchanged: 3 stars only in the green).
4. **Locked tiles answer the tap** (js/ui/player/home.js): tapping the locked Match day tile shows the sentence the
   locked page uses ("Win a star in Big Match to open it."), the tile reads "Finish Big Match", the gate node's fold
   opens if it was folded, and the pulse remains.
5. **The home screen** (home.js): every chapter title is visible (sighted and screen-reader alike); the current
   chapter is expanded; another open chapter folds behind its title as a native details element; a locked chapter is a
   lock and its title, no nodes. `homeWords` counts everything visible and the budget test moved from 25 to 40 words
   with its rationale updated, so hiding text can no longer satisfy it. First-screen padlocks: 18 to about 5.
6. **The softener** (play.js `revealNote`): "Hard one. Pros miss it too." never fires when the miss was locking in at
   the start. Verified live.
7. **Warm-up disclosure** (play.js): the first set's two uncounted reps say "Warm-up. Stars count from the next play."
   the same way Try again discloses practice.

## Tier 2: deletions

8. **Card ratings** (js/ui/player/card.js): `skillRatings` (0-99, a new player read 45 four times) became
   `chapterStars`: earned Road stars over their ceiling per chapter ("★ 0/12 Defend"). The card's metal follows the
   level at the old ranks' thresholds (`cardMetal(level)`).
9. **Ranks and badges** (js/rewards.js and consumers): ranks deleted everywhere (levels remain); badges cut 16 → 5,
   every one earned by stars (Spot on, Hat-trick, Perfect set, All-rounder, Collector). Old saved badge ids drop away
   in `normalizeRewards`, the established precedent. The level-up screen lost its rank line and ribbon.
10. **XP demoted from the top bar** (js/ui/player/shell.js): the Player top bar is identity only (figure, nickname,
    card, settings); the level and XP live on the card and at Full time. (Levels kept; kit colours stay level-gated.)
11. **author.js removed** (2,131 lines + 245 lines of CSS): `#/author` was reachable only by typed URL, tested by
    nothing, and shipped to every phone. Scenario authoring is `data/scenarios/_example.json` + `npm run check`
    (README's "To add a scenario" rewritten). The route falls to the existing coming-soon card.
12. **One scoring language**: Match day's big word is the star word (Spot on / Great / Close / Not yet), the
    Hot/Warm/Cold vocabulary, its shapes and the "Best hot streak" row are gone (`bestHotStreak` deleted), and the
    ring (Match day and the rep-2 glow alike) turns green in the right area with the tips reworded to say so. The
    pass options wear the spec's four words: the engine's `offside`/`danger` labels display as "✗ Cut out" (ranked
    after a plain cut-out; the offside outcome keeps its whistle, its word and its drawn line).
13. **The Coach door** (kickoff.js, shell.js): the "Coach or parent?" link left the kick-off screen (one tappable
    thing: Play). The settings-sheet door now asks a grown-up sum first; a wrong answer clears and stays in Player
    mode. Verified live both ways. Mode persistence is unchanged (the gate guards entry).
14. **Stage labels** (strings.js, play.js, pass.js): "Small game: 3 v 2" / "Now 6 v 5!" left the role card; the pitch
    shows how many players there are. The stage still rides on the card as a data attribute, which the staging checks
    in the mount tests now read.
15. **Copy pass** (data/principles.json, data/tutorial.json): the seven two-conditional sticker lines are one idea
    each; "strip" / "in-between strip" (the renamed half-space) left the kid register, and P1's sticker is "Hard to
    Mark". The coach-facing standard register and the S7 build-out-line entry (not on the Road) are unchanged.
16. **Kit step** (kickoff.js): "Make it yours" shows locked colours with their unlock levels (the kit locker's own
    rendering), so the choice reads as a collection to earn instead of a one-swatch section.

## Audit items measured wrong or declined, left alone deliberately

- **Cast fallbacks kept** (`decisiveIds`/`stillIds`): removing them cost authored drill m2-04-b2-st its only
  smaller-game stage, which the content gate rightly refuses. The real hazard was fixed instead: the `prep.decisive`
  memo is keyed per stage.
- **`difficulty` field kept**: the audit said it had no reader left; grep found three (easy-first ordering in Player
  sets, recall-rep ranking, generated-drill difficulty).
- **Yield tables kept** (`PASS_YIELD`/`SPOT_YIELD`): the runtime-memo replacement risks 120-150 ms dead generator
  calls in a kid's first set on a slow phone; the tables only cost maintenance.
- **Board declutter/occlusion kept**: legible shirt numbers carry the "their number 7" naming language the whole kid
  copy depends on; measured cost about 1 ms a frame; no jank observed live.
- **NaN-to-null context change skipped**: a behavior-preserving version is a no-op, and a behavior-changing one
  alters gated edge semantics across 17 rules for no user-visible gain (the quality gates already exclude the only
  frames that reach it).
- **drawLabel measurement rewrite skipped**: performance-only, no observed defect, heavy test surface (1,668 placed
  labels asserted); flagged as possible follow-up.
- **kidscore's 13 per-rule key thresholds untouched**: collapsing them to a shared slack risks shifting the measured
  star distribution; the audit listed it as available, not recommended.
