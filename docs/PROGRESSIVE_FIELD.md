# Progressive field, player figures and a ball you can see (spec and build contract)

**Owner feedback, 2026-09-28:**
1. The field is too clogged. Kids don't need every player in every exercise: start simple and build up to the full field.
2. Circles as players are boring: use a simple character.
3. The ball is impossible to see: it doesn't need to be life size; make it easy to spot.

Binding for the build that follows, like [KID_REDESIGN.md](KID_REDESIGN.md) (whose rules still apply: word budgets, reading age, stars not grades, colour + shape, reduced motion, 44 px targets, Coach mode unchanged).

## 1. Stages: small game → bigger game → full match

| Stage | Players shown | Who | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| `small` | 3–6 in total (2 v 1, 2 v 2, 3 v 2 …) | the learner, the ball carrier(s) and the players the lesson is about | fits the cast (both axes), so figures are big |
| `medium` | 6–12 (5 v 4, 6 v 6 …) | the learner's unit plus the attack or defence around the ball | fits the cast |
| `full` | all 22 | today's scene | as today (length crop on phones, spotlight on key players) |

- **Hidden players are not drawn and not scored.** A staged rep is played and judged on a **reduced frame** that holds only the cast. Every star, ring and sentence depends only on players the kid can see.
- **Same lesson at every size (the gate).** A stage is used for a rep only if, on its reduced frame at the freeze:
  - **spot reps:** the ghost scores ≥ 90; the rep's primary principle's rule is weighted ≥ 2 with s ≥ 0.9 at the ghost; the ghost is within `sameAnswer` (4 m [D]) of the full-game ghost; standing still at the start scores < 70 (unless the drill is an authored "hold" drill).
  - **pass reps:** the best receiver is the same as in the full game; at least one other shown option is labelled risky or cut-out, or the best is clearly ahead (a real choice); the shown options are 3–5 teammates.
  - If a stage fails, the cast grows (next most relevant players) up to that stage's cap; still failing, the rep falls back to the next bigger stage. `full` always passes.
- **Stage label:** count ours v theirs in the cast ("3 v 2"); `full` = "11 v 11". Goalkeepers count only when in the cast.
- **Offside:** when the lesson involves the offside line (an attacker ahead of the ball, principles F4/B2/P5 or the `offside`/`pin` rules weighted ≥ 2), the defenders who set the line are in the cast.
- **Clip:** every player who has the ball during the clip (timeline carriers, override players who receive) is in the cast.

**Built** (waves 1-2 and integration, 2026-09-28). `js/engine/cast.js` stages every rep; `play.js` and `pass.js` watch, freeze, judge and reveal a staged rep on reduced frames only (ARCHITECTURE §5.16, §5.17). Beyond this section the gate also asks that the full game's best spot scores S on the reduced frame, that the praise at the answer is the full game's (3 deep), and for pass reps that the best's pass is safe in the full game, that no receiver rises to 3 stars and that every offside call is unchanged; the cast always shows the players a drill scripts or names in its kid words (`keepIdsOf`) and the players the judging reads (first defender, mark, the presser on the ball); the offside setters come in whenever a call in the cast would change without them. Integration fix: the level line in a small game with nobody else of the back line in it fell back to YOUR own base, so "in line with your other defenders" and a "Your defenders" line named players nobody saw (m1-02, m3-11); now there is no line to hold there (`rules/_util.js backLineRef`), and a test gathers every line, Why? sheet and cue a staged authored rep can show at spots all round the answer and checks each group it names against the cast. Later (review): a pass rep's smaller games take the most compact cast that passes the gate and still teaches the lesson (`passCompact`: the relevance order's first cast, the compact order's and, in the small game, a search of up to `passSearch` casts, judged by how far they spill over `passBox`, 36 × 22 m, about a phone held upright's board at 13 px/m; ARCHITECTURE §5.17), since the camera fits the cast and a cast with a decoy at each touchline drew its figures no bigger than the full match's. Measured stage availability [M]: authored drills and mirrors (72) small 40 (56 %), medium 72 (100 %); generated spot drills (501) small 68 %, medium 97 %; generated pass drills (734: every learnable role x every pass lesson, seeds 1-8; before the compact cast → now) small 34 → 42 % (full-backs 2 → 20 %, centre-backs 16 → 29, the #6 25 → 36, the #8s 44 → 45, wingers 71 → 74, the #9 38 → 41 %), medium 97 → 98 %, the lesson still taught in 76 % of small and 87 % of medium reps.

## 2. Stage plan: how a set builds up

Per Road set of 5 reps, from the node's stars **before** the set:

| Node stars | Reps 1–5 |
|---|---|
| 0 | small, small, small, medium, medium |
| 1 | small, small, medium, medium, full |
| 2 | small, medium, medium, full, full |
| 3 | medium, full, full, full, full |

- `#/play/first` (onboarding): small, small, small.
- Quick "Who's open?" (`#/pass`): the 1-star plan.
- "Try again" keeps the first try's stage. Recall reps use the plan's stage for their slot.
- Match day: full (with figures and the new ball). Coach mode: full field, discs (and, since wave 2, the new ball: see §4).
- The role card names the role only. (It named the stage in a few words, "Small game: 3 v 2", until the 2026-10-01 audit cut the line: the pitch already shows how many players there are. The stage still rides on the card as data for the tests.)

**Built** (`road.js stagePlan`, `STAGE_PLANS`; `buildSet`, `buildQuickPassSet` and `buildFirstSet` tag every rep's `stage`, look up to `stageLook` (2) more drills while the set is short of reps that can be played small, and order the set so the most reps play at their slot's stage; `fulltime.js` "Next time: bigger games" when a node's stars rose; the stage line and `stageLine` went with the 2026-10-01 audit). Deviations: a rep that cannot be staged at its slot's stage plays the next bigger one that can (never smaller). The onboarding set takes the easy drills of your group that can be played small in your own position first (integration fix: passing over a defender's easiest left-back drills, which need a bigger game, once dealt all three in the left centre-back's shoes). Review fix (wave 2): the onboarding set was never anyone's "last set", so a defender's and a striker's first Road set dealt 2 of its 3 drills straight back (the worked example among them, scored for real) and the next node's recall rep brought the tutorial back again; now `buildFirstSet`, given the app, makes its reps the profile's last set (`startSet(app, 'first', reps)`, nothing counted) and they stay in it through the first Road set after it, which takes the chapter's other ideas and more reps in other positions first. Measured [M] with the real generators and the app's seeds (a new player's onboarding set, then three Road sets at 1 star): the next two Road sets deal none of the onboarding drills (before the fix the very next one dealt 1-2 of the 3 for every group), and one comes back in the third; `tests/road-sets-*.test.js` checks the first node's set for every group. Measured [M] (6 seeds x 4 groups, every spot and pass node's first set, the quick set and the onboarding set, the real generators; 2026-09-28): small slots played small: spot nodes 731 of 768 (95 %), onboarding 66 of 72 (92 %: a striker's third rep is a bigger game, only two easy striker drills can be played small), pass nodes 209 of 288 (73 %: defenders 27 of 72, midfielders 52, strikers 60, wingers 70), the quick set 34 of 48 (71 %: defenders 1 of 12); bigger-game slots: spot 526 of 528, pass 171 of 192, quick 48 of 48; every set had a small game except 8 of 96 pass sets and 5 of 24 quick sets (all defenders' or strikers'). With the compact pass casts of §1 (6 seeds x 4 groups again, every pass node's first set and the quick set: 408 small slots, before → after): small slots played small 294 → 333, defenders' pass nodes 30 → 49 of 90 and their quick sets 1 → 4 of 12, bigger-game slots 248 → 256 of 288, sets with no small game 16 → 7 of 144. The sweep (`tests/road-sets-*.test.js`, seed 1) reports it per group; on 2026-09-29, all four groups: the 0-star plan and the onboarding set small 219 of 236 and bigger 148 of 152, the 3-star plan and the quick set small 8 of 8, bigger 84 of 84, full 308 of 308 (defenders and midfielders 52 of 59 small, wingers 58, strikers 57).

## 3. Engine: `js/engine/cast.js` (pure)

```js
export const STAGES = Object.freeze(['small', 'medium', 'full']);
export const CAST_DEFAULTS = { small: { min: 3, max: 6 }, medium: { min: 6, max: 12 }, sameAnswer: 4, stillMax: 70, ... };  // [D]

/** The players to show and score for a stage, most relevant first. */
castFor(frame, { learnerId, base, ghost, principles, stage, clipIds = [], params }) → { ids: string[], label: string, ours: number, theirs: number }
//   relevance: the learner; the ball carrier and clipIds; the context's firstDefender, secondDefender, markTarget and
//   dangerousAttacker; players cued by the rules weighted ≥ 2 at the ghost (rule.cue(ctx)); the offside-line setters when
//   the lesson involves them; then the nearest players to the ghost, the ball and the learner's start, keeping both teams
//   represented; capped by the stage.

reduceFrame(frame, ids) → Frame   // only those players; ball, possession, tags kept; carrierId kept only when in ids

stageSpotDrill(scenario, stage, { formations, principles, params }) → { stage, cast, ghost, base, tol, gates } | null
stagePassDrill(drill, stage, { formations, params }) → { stage, cast, rating, answer: { best, accept } } | null
//   null: this stage can't teach this drill (the gate failed even after growing the cast)
bestStage(item, wanted, opts) → the staged result at `wanted`, or the next bigger stage that passes (never null: full passes)
```

`scripts/check-scenarios.mjs` reports, for every authored drill, which stages pass (small / medium / full) and fails CI only on a drill with no small **or** medium stage and no documented reason (`"stages": { "note": "…" }` in the scenario).

**Built** as above, plus `stagesOf` (every stage at once), `castLabel`, `clipIdsOf`, `keepIdsOf`, `kidTextsOf`, `castExtent` (how far a cast spills over `passBox`: the compact pass casts of §1) and the shared tap-target helpers in `js/engine/passtargets.js`; a scenario's `"stages"` block takes `note` and `keep` (players every cast shows, mirrored with the drill). `npm run check` also fails a cast that hides a player the drill names and a primary idea unmet at the answer (unless noted); integration: `validateScenario` checks the `"stages"` block too (`scenario.js stagesErrors`, the same as the check's), so `#/author` flags it, and the editor keeps the block on download.

## 4. Board: figures, the ball and the camera (`js/ui/board.js`, `js/ui/figures.js`)

```js
createBoard(container, { orientation, labels, figures /* true in Player mode, false (discs) in Coach mode */ })
board.render(frame, opts)          // unchanged; a reduced frame simply draws only its cast (absent players are hidden)
board.setCamera(rect | null)       // rect { x0, x1, y0, y1 } world metres: fit that area on both axes (keep the box's aspect,
                                   // clamp to the pitch + margin, never smaller than CAMERA_MIN 24 × 16 m [D]); eases unless
                                   // reduced motion; null = today's behaviour (setFocus length crop)
// js/ui/figures.js (DOM helper, no engine imports)
drawFigure(parent, { shirt, edge, ink, shorts, socks, number, skin, hair, hairStyle, gk, facing: 1 | -1, size }) → SVGGElement
figureLook(playerId, team) → { skin, hair, hairStyle }   // deterministic variety per player
```

**Figures** ("tabletop figure"):
- the team-coloured **base disc** under the feet (keeps today's readability from above and the ≥ 44 px hit area; wave 2: about the figure's shoulder width across at every scale, so a zoomed-in figure never stands on a plate, while the hit area stays ≥ 44 px) and an **upright figure** on it: head with a hair shape, shirt with the number in the kit's ink colour, shorts, socks, simple arms and legs; about 2.4 token radii tall; faceless (sporty, not babyish, KID_REDESIGN rule 7);
- upright on screen in both orientations; flips left/right to face the ball;
- a two-pose run cycle while moving, still when standing; no animation under reduced motion;
- variety per player id: 5 skin tones, 5 hair colours, 4 hair styles; goalkeepers in a distinct kit with gloves;
- our team in the kid's kit palette (`--kit-us*`), theirs in the dark kit (`--kit-them*`): the lightness contrast is kept (colour-blind safe);
- YOU: a gold glow on the base and the YOU/nickname tag above the head (as built, every render the tag goes where it covers least round YOUR head: over it, slid, lifted, beside it or under YOUR feet, in the largest of three forms that fits: the nickname pill, a compact "YOU" chevron, a chevron alone); the carrier has the ball at their feet (as built, on the side of the feet that covers nobody's head or number: beside the boots on the side they face first, else at the toes, else beside the other boot);
- Coach mode keeps the discs (`figures: false`), but has the new ball too (wave 2: the owner's "impossible to see" was about every pitch): bigger, outline, halo; a carried ball sits at the edge of its carrier's disc (drawn where it is, it hid the disc and its code). YOUR tag on the discs stays as it was: over the token, moved only while the ball would cover it (`discTagPlacement`).

**Ball:**
- drawn above every figure, never hidden under one;
- at least 18 CSS px across on a 375 px phone at every stage (bigger when zoomed), classic white with dark patches and a thick dark outline;
- a bright halo ring (e.g. #FFD400) that pulses gently, plus a ground shadow; the halo stays (static) under reduced motion;
- a short fading trail while in flight; when carried it sits at the carrier's feet (the side they face first, another side when that one would cover a head or a number: see Figures);
- contrast ≥ 3:1 against the pitch in both themes.

**Built** (wave 1: figures, the ball, the camera; wave 2: the base disc in proportion to the figure, YOUR tag clear of the ball, the new ball in Coach mode; later review fixes: YOUR tag where it covers least, close quarters, the halo by the ring, the grass while the camera eases). Deviations (ARCHITECTURE §5.8):
- **Coach mode** gets the easy-to-see ball too, carried just outside its carrier's disc toward the goal it plays to (else across or back, whichever keeps it off the other discs' codes: `discBallAt`, `discCarryDir`), since the owner said the ball was impossible to see and, drawn where it is, it covered the carrier's code. Everything else on the discs is as before, YOUR tag included (`discTagPlacement`: over the token, moved only while the ball would cover it).
- **The best-spot ring** (figures) is drawn over the figures and under the ball (a pressing spot lies by the ball and the carrier, and under them it read as the halo). While the ball's full halo would reach the ring, the halo shrinks to a thin, still ring round the ball (a yellow ring the size of the best-spot ring read as the ring itself; hidden, as it first was, the ball lost its cue right at the answer), and a ball inside the ring grows the ring just clear of it (`ghostFit`).
- **YOUR tag** (figures) goes, every render, where it covers least round YOUR head (over it, slid left or right, lifted, beside the head or under YOUR feet: `tagPlacement`), weighing other players' heads and numbers and the zone just over them (a tag there reads as theirs), the ball, the labels, the ring and the view's edge; the nickname pill over the head whenever that is clear, a compact "YOU" chevron or a chevron alone only in a crowd; never more than about a tag's width from YOUR head, and it moves only for something clearly better, so it never flickers. (It first moved only for the ball, and covered another player's head or number on 55 of 199 boards of an end-to-end run.)
- **Close quarters** (figures, display only): figures standing so close that one hides another's head or shirt number are drawn up to 1 m apart (`declutter`: never YOU, and nothing the engine, the scoring or a drag sees moves), sliding there; a carried ball sits on the side of the feet that hides nobody's head or number (`carrySpot`), sliding between sides, never jumping.
- **The camera** keeps room over its rect for a figure and YOUR tag at its zoom and may reach past the pitch's margin at the top of the screen for it (`standHeight`). While it is on, and through every frame of its ease, the grass covers all the board shows (the window, the strips a window of another aspect leaves, and `cameraSurround` more); and it measures the board again just after the mode's own page change (a microtask), so under reduced motion the view lands once, on the right window.

Integration: a label kept off YOU (`clear: 'you'`: "Best spot", the pass labels, a cue's name) now also keeps off the other figures and the ball, weighed less than YOU (zoomed in on a small game, a presser's "Best spot" sat across the winger's head); YOUR tag is slid back inside the view when YOU stand at its edge (the pill was cut by the edge); a player left out of a reduced frame keeps no state (last rep's learner stayed `.is-learner` while hidden). ## 5. Player integration

- `js/ui/player/road.js`: `stagePlan(nodeStars, { first, count })` (pure, §2); `buildSet` tags each rep with its wanted `stage`.
- `play.js` / `pass.js`: at rep start, stage the rep with `bestStage` (engine), then play the clip, freeze, judge and reveal on reduced frames; `board.setCamera` fits the cast plus the ball, YOU's start and the best spot for small/medium; spotlight only at full; the role card shows the stage words; figures on.
- `matchday.js`: figures on, full field.
- `card.js`, the kit locker, `kickoff.js` and the home top bar show the kid's own figure in their kit (`drawFigure`).
- All new words go in the modules' `STRINGS` (the copy test checks them).

**Built** (wave 2, then review fixes). Deviations:
- **Two cameras in "Find your spot".** A small or bigger game has the clip's camera, one rect for the whole clip (the cast, the ball, YOUR start and the best spot on frames every 0.5 s), steady through the watch and "See what happens" and easing between reps; and at the whistle the board eases in on the freeze (`play.js answerCamera`: the cast and the ball at the freeze, YOUR start and the best spot), where the kid decides and sees the answer (measured before it was built: a rect for the freeze alone would be 0.73 of the clip's area at the median, under 0.6 for 9 of 56 staged authored reps); YOU going to its edge widens it, head and tag included. Words about the sideline ("has the ball by the sideline") keep it in view (`touchlineBy`). "Who's open?" does the same: at the whistle a small or bigger game eases in on the freeze (`pass.js chooseCamera`: its players, the ball and the teammates a tap can pick), a pass into space off that view widens it as the ball goes (`flightCamera`), and the reveal eases onto the play (`revealCamera`). With the compact pass casts (§1) the choice's figures on a phone grew: in the end-to-end re-check small pass games went from 40.6 to 51.6 px figures at the median (7.2 → 11.2 px/m; at most 45 px 4 of 5 → 2 of 7), bigger ones 45.8 → 48.8 px (ARCHITECTURE §5.17).
- **The card waits for the camera.** Both games' cards ("You're the left back", "You've got the ball", with the game's size) are placed and shown only once the camera has landed (`cardSettleMs` after it: placed while it eased in, a card covered YOU, YOUR tag or the ball for a moment), then hold their usual time. They go where they cover nobody, where the board will draw everyone: the top or bottom of the pitch, the middle, the tallest band between the players, each centred and then slid to the left or right edge; else wherever they cover least of YOU and the ball, then the player on the ball ("Find your spot") or the teammates to pass to ("Who's open?"), then everyone else (`cardSpot` with `cardWeights`). They were always in the middle, where a camera centred on the play put YOU or the ball under them. Measured [M] (the end-to-end re-check, a 40 ms monitor on every card of 64 reps): shown while the camera still eased on 62 reps before the close-quarters fixes and 28 after (over more than a quarter of another figure 27, then 10); on the code as it is now (2026-09-29), with the card waiting for the camera, none of the 64, none over YOU, YOUR tag or the ball at any sample, and 1 over more than a quarter of another figure.
- **"Best spot"** goes by the ring where it covers nobody (over it, under it, beside it, running off to one side), else a short callout with a line from the ring (`play.js bestSpotPlace`, with where the board draws everyone); a ring right by YOU once had no words at all (9 of 30 misses in the verifier's run, then none).
- **Whom the words mean.** A group the picture shows more than once is named by the shirt number drawn ("their number 7": `play.js repSpeaker`, `nameSpecific`; "Who's open?" `passSpeaker`, `revealWords`), from who the words are known to mean (the player on the ball where they say so, else the player the rule or reason behind them is about, else the drill's scripted player), never a guess; "Who's open?" says a plural group shown once in the singular ("their midfielder"); a sentence that cannot say whom it means is not shown (a question asks the default one; the brief is shown as written: ROADMAP). Measured [M] (the verifiers' screens in headless Chrome, a singular group word with more than one of that group drawn): 40 screens in the first run, 37 in the second before its fixes, 8 after them (all "Who's open?" reveals, before `passSpeaker`); now none (one "Find your spot" reveal still said a plural, "past their midfielders", with one of them drawn: ROADMAP), and no number named a shirt nobody drawn wears.
- Reps are staged ahead of time (the set's first while the pitch gets ready, the next during the reveal, and in "Find your spot" after a miss its Try again twin just after the reveal shows; "Who's open?"'s Try again replays the same staged freeze), reusing what the set builder staged (`road.js stagedRep`).
